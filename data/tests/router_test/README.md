# Upland Breeding Bird Survey — Connemara & West Mayo, 2019–2021

## Overview

This dataset records point-count observations of upland breeding birds across the
mountains and blanket bog of western Ireland (Connemara, County Galway, and west
County Mayo). It was compiled to support long-term monitoring of species
associated with unenclosed upland habitats and to provide a baseline against
which future change can be measured.

In total, 1,000 observations of upland breeding birds were recorded across 25
survey squares over three breeding seasons.

## Methods

Surveys followed a standard point-count protocol. At each survey point, a single
observer recorded all birds seen or heard during a fixed five-minute period,
between 06:00 and 10:00 in dry conditions with low wind. Each square was visited
twice per breeding season. Location was taken with a handheld GPS, and air
temperature and elevation were noted at each point. Species are recorded using
four-letter codes; see `codebook.csv` for the code list and for the meaning of
every column in `observations.csv`.

## Coverage

- **Temporal:** breeding seasons from March 2019 to September 2021.
- **Spatial:** western Ireland — the uplands of Connemara (County Galway) and
  west County Mayo.
